## Brand & Style

The design system projects high-precision athletic engineering, kinetic vitality, and sports-science clarity. Targeted at elite performers, endurance athletes, and data-driven biohackers, the visual language merges the sleek technical depth of mission control telemetry with the hyper-focused clarity of high-output human performance.

The aesthetic framework operates at the intersection of technical minimalism and electric futurism. It relies on deep obsidian structural foundations, luminous cyan data traces, cold-filtered telemetry panels, and crisp vector delineation. Surfaces evoke dark carbon composite and anti-reflective cockpit optics. Interactions trigger calibrated, hyper-responsive optical emissions: glowing accents indicate kinetic thresholds, biometric telemetry scales with sharp precision, and interfaces eliminate unnecessary ornamental fluff in favor of razor-sharp data legibility and effortless glanceability during high-intensity output.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is anchored in cold, crystalline photonics balanced over three deep obsidian substrate layers:

- **Substrates & Containers**:
  - `bg-base`: `#080b11` (Deep obsidian backdrop, void-level floor).
  - `bg-surface`: `#0f141d` (Elevated telemetry module and panel background).
  - `bg-surface-raised`: `#161e2b` (Active cards, interactive states, and popovers).
  
- **Accents & Telemetry**:
  - `primary`: `#00f0ff` (Hyper-pure electric cyan used for primary metrics, active thresholds, focus states, and primary call-to-actions).
  - `secondary`: `#38bdf8` (Ice sky blue for secondary progress indicators, trend vectors, and categorical graphs).
  - `tertiary`: `#7dd3fc` (Glacier frost blue for tertiary metadata, interactive secondary toggles, and neutral data points).

- **Structural Outlines & Borders**:
  - `border-subtle`: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)` (Passive separation grids).
  - `border-crisp`: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)` (Standard card outlines and module dividers).
  - `border-active`: `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.45)` (Hovered, focused, and active states).

- **Typography & Data Tones**:
  - `text-primary`: `#f0f6fc` (Cool stark white for maximum contrast headlines and real-time readouts).
  - `text-secondary`: `#8ba2b9` (Cool slate for supporting metrics, units, and structural metadata).
  - `text-tertiary`: `#4d657d` (Subdued slate for timestamps, inactive states, and grid calibration marks).
  - `status-alert`: `#ff3864` (Critical biometric warnings and anaerobic ceiling limits).
  - `status-success`: `#00ffa3` (Target output achieved, optimal recovery zones).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a deliberate dual-engine strategy:

1. **Sora (Geometric Display)**: Directs top-level macro-information, category anchors, and section headers. Its wide, geometric stance and curved apertures deliver a forward-looking, high-performance athletic tone without feeling decorative.
2. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Engine)**: Drives the complete telemetry layer, body text, unit descriptions, and real-time metric updates. The monospaced cadence enforces visual rhythm, prevents jitter during rapid numerical counter mutations, and ensures absolute vertical and tabular alignment across split screens and dashboards.

Apply `label-caps` in uppercase format to denote technical parameters, telemetry channels, hardware connection indicators, and sensor categories.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on a 12-column modular grid (collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile) engineered for low-cognitive-load scanning in dynamic environments. 

- **Density & Cadence**: Component gaps and interior paddings leverage a binary 4px base increment (`0.25rem`). Data displays require tight grouping (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to link numerical values with units and delta badges, while functional modules separate with `space-lg` or `space-xl` to maintain clean spatial parsing.
- **Form-Factor Adaptations**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin`: `1rem`, `gutter`: `1rem`. Dense single-column telemetry stack. Primary continuous charts lock to edge-to-edge bleed with inset labels.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `margin-tablet`: `2rem`, `gutter`: `1rem`. Dual-pane split featuring active target metric on the left and historical cadence/lactate curves on the right.
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, `margin-desktop`: `3rem`, `gutter-desktop`: `1.5rem`. Multi-channel telemetry dashboard with fixed system health telemetry sidebar, fluid analytical core, and modular athlete control dock.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons diffuse organic drop shadows entirely in favor of an engineered **Atmospheric Layering and Optical Luminescence** system:

1. **Layer Hierarchy**:
   - **Ground Layer (`#080b11`)**: The master background substrate. Never holds text directly without a panel enclosure or terminal rail.
   - **Panel Substrate (`#0f141d`)**: Elevated by crisp `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)` hairline borders (1px). Surfaces are matte and absorptive.
   - **Floating Telemetry & Overlays (`#161e2b`)**: Active interactive layers and critical alert modules utilize ultra-subtle tinted specular backdrops: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` combined with a background of `rgba(22, 30, 43, 0.85)`.

2. **Electric Luminescence (The Photonic Edge)**:
   - High-priority interactive triggers, recording pulses, or zone violations emit a concentrated neon cyan glow using dual-stage box shadows:
     - `box-shadow: 0 0 1px 1px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.8), 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35);`
   - Data points along telemetry line graphs emit ambient cyan haloes (`filter: drop-shadow(0 0 6px #00f0ff)`), highlighting active thresholds against the carbon backdrop.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a disciplined, technical profile centered on **Level 1 (Soft)** curvature:

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, cards, metric panels) use an exacting corner radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- Larger modal envelopes and primary telemetry docks employ `0.5rem` (8px).
- Large containers rarely exceed `0.75rem` (12px), keeping the aesthetic anchored in rugged digital instrumentation rather than consumer softness.

Small tactical elements—such as telemetry status indicators, tag badges, and live heartbeat tick marks—feature chamfered, architectural silhouettes or strictly defined 4px edges that mimic milled hardware and carbon panels.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Kinetic Button**: Solid `#00f0ff` fill with high-contrast `#080b11` text set in `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. Hover induces a sharp cyan photonic bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Telemetry Button**: Background `#161e2b`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`, text `#00f0ff`. Hover shifts border to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)` with a faint interior wash of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Ghost/Auxiliary Action**: Transparent background with crisp `#8ba2b9` text, switching to `#f0f6fc` on hover with a single cyan indicator tick mark on the left edge.

### Chips & Badges
- Constructed with a height of `24px`, using `bg-surface-raised` (`#161e2b`), a hairline cyan border (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)`), and `label-caps` typography.
- Status badges include a 6px glowing vector pip (e.g., `#00f0ff` for recording, `#00ffa3` for optimal recovery, `#ff3864` for threshold breach).

### Telemetry Cards & Panels
- Background: `#0f141d`. Border: 1px continuous hairline `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.
- Header area features a distinct structural top border or technical header bracket with a sub-label in `label-caps` (`#4d657d`).
- Interior space leverages `space-md` padding. Primary values feature `telemetry-metric-lg` typography in `#f0f6fc` paired with a small floating unit marker in `#38bdf8`.

### Input Fields
- Dark recessed background (`#080b11`), framed by 1px `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)` border.
- Text: `JetBrains Mono` 14px in `#f0f6fc`. Placeholder text in `#4d657d`.
- Focus state: Border accelerates to `#00f0ff` with an inner hairline highlight and an external glow of `0 0 8px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`.

### Lists & Telemetry Feeds
- Alternating or continuous separator lines built from `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`.
- Numeric row counters, split times, and athlete data coordinates format entirely with tabular lining monospaced digits to preserve strict column alignment.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radios
- Square 16px footprint with a strict 2px radius. Border: 1px `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)`.
- Checked state: `#00f0ff` solid fill with an obsidian checkmark or a centered luminous cyan LED pip.

### Specialized Fitness Telemetry Components
- **Biometric Gauge / Heart Rate Arc**: Circular vector tracks in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.1)` with the active segment pulsing in `#00f0ff` and high zones crossing into `#ff3864`.
- **Wattage & Cadence Graphs**: Vector sparklines drawn with a 1.5px `#00f0ff` stroke and an underlying gradient fill fading from `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)` to transparent.
