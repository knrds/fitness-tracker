## Brand & Style

This design system channels raw kinetic street-sport culture through aggressive high-vis chartreuse, high-tension variable typography, and aerodynamic pill-shaped telemetry cards. Built for an iOS-first environment, it captures the electric velocity of elite nocturnal athletics, urban speed trials, and high-cadence biometric tracking.

The visual style fuses radical minimalism with high-contrast performance brutalism:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** A pitch-black void engineered with deep midnight navy surfaces that eliminate interface glare during high-intensity conditions.
- **Electric Contrast:** Electric chartreuse paired with cool cyan-mint accents that slice through deep backgrounds to instantly broadcast live telemetry and personal records.
- **Kinetic Stance:** Dynamic forward-leaning variable typography that conveys speed, velocity, and forward momentum across stats and HUD-style metrics.
- **Aerodynamic Anatomy:** Oversized capsule contours and extruded pill cards engineered for rapid thumb targeting while on the move.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for instantaneous biometric recognition, rapid scan-readiness, and low-light visual acuity:

- **Primary (`#CCFF00`):** Electric Street Chartreuse. Reserved strictly for primary action targets, active motion timers, target cadence zones, and breakthrough personal records.
- **Secondary (`#34D399`):** Punchy Velocity Mint. Used for biometric recovery indices, completed intervals, positive trend lines, and sub-metric telemetry trackers.
- **Tertiary (`#0F1C33`):** Aerodynamic Cobalt. Forms elevated visual containers, telemetry surfaces, and segmented selector track beds.
- **Neutral (`#080E1E`):** Midnight Abyss. The core viewport canvas, absorbing ambient screen emissions and maximizing relative luminance for foreground telemetry.

### Semantic & State Variants
- **Critical Alert / Peak Effort (`#FF2E54`):** Anaerobic heart-rate spikes, threshold warnings, and system disconnects.
- **Inactive / Surface Border (`#1B2E52`):** Structural micro-borders delineating nested data pods without producing visual noise.
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Pure white-gray for maximum contrast against deep obsidian navy.
- **Text Secondary (`#64748B`):** Muted slate for peripheral units of measurement (e.g., `BPM`, `KM/H`, `W/KG`).

## Typography

Typography functions as visual mechanical propulsion within this system:
- **Headlines & Big Metrics (`Anybody`):** Rendered with a high-tension athletic presence. Hero counters, metrics, and module titles use heavy weights (700 to 900) paired with tighter letter tracking. When indicating live kinetic output (e.g., active pace, speedometers, split alerts), display text leans into subtle italicized variable axes to induce physical urgency.
- **Body & Data Infrastructure (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers clean monospace-adjacent clarity while retaining humanistic geometric edges. Numerical metrics display with tabular figures to avoid layout jitter during live telemetry updates.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-labels always render in all-caps with generous tracking (+0.08em) to anchor the high-tech cockpit feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized for high-movement interaction models and single-hand accessibility on mobile platforms:

- **Layout Structure:** 4-column fluid layout on mobile screens expanding to 8 columns on tablets and 12 columns on large displays. The outer margin is pinned to `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports, aligning with standard iOS safe zones.
- **Thumb-Zone Optimization:** Crucial interactive elements (split triggers, workout terminators, pace toggles) are prioritized along the bottom 40% of the viewport canvas.
- **Rhythmic Stacking:** Spacing relies on compact `0.5rem` steps for sub-metric clusters and expands to `1.5rem` - `2.25rem` gaps between macro-telemetry sections to provide quick glance separation during physical motion.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional light-source drop shadows in favor of deep-space optical glows and high-contrast tonal containment:

- **Base Layer (Canvas):** Pure `#080E1E` creates the deepest recessive plane.
- **Container Tiers:** Structural cards use `#0F1C33` with a 1px perimeter outline (`#1B2E52` at 60% opacity) to provide edge definition against the canvas without heavy drop shadows.
- **Kinetic Luminescence:** Interactive elements, such as active states and target heart-rate zones, use localized perimeter glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.28)`.
- **Floating HUD Layers:** Modals and bottom split drawers incorporate high-density backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) over a semi-translucent `#080E1EB3` surface, allowing background athletic video feeds or maps to register faintly underneath.

## Shapes

The design system uses an aerodynamic, pill-dominant visual geometry:

- **Roundedness Scale Level 3:** Core cards, floating panels, and action triggers feature oversized capsule forms. Primary cards adopt large radii (20px to 24px), while buttons, chips, and progress caps adopt full geometric pill structures (9999px).
- **Segmented Enclosures:** Stat blocks sit within rounded containment shells that visually mimic aerodynamic racing fins, automotive clusters, and athletic eyewear ergonomics.
- **Progressive Pill Caps:** All linear progress indicators, split rings, and volume sliders terminate in complete half-circle caps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic Trigger:** Fully rounded pill container (`rounded-full`) filled with `#CCFF00`, featuring `#080E1E` bold typography. On touch-down/press, it contracts mechanically to `scale(0.96)` with a radiant 12px chartreuse glow.
- **Secondary Capsule:** `#0F1C33` background with a 1.5px `#1B2E52` outline and high-contrast `#F8FAFC` body text.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background featuring high-vis text in `#CCFF00` or `#34D399` alongside an italicized directional chevron icon.

### Telemetry Cards & Metric Pods
- Elevated `#0F1C33` containers with 20px–24px corner radii.
- Designed with high vertical padding (`space-lg`) and asymmetric data placement: small top-left uppercase tracker labels (`label-caps`), flanked by giant tabular numeral readings (`stat-counter`) spanning the center, and a trailing dynamic trend badge on the bottom right.

### Chips & HUD Filters
- Compact pill-shaped selectors (height: 36px).
- Inactive state: `#080E1E` fill with subtle `#1B2E52` border.
- Active state: `#CCFF00` surface with solid `#080E1E` text, communicating instantaneous mode shifts (e.g., `HEART RATE`, `CADENCE`, `POWER WATTS`).

### Sliders & Kinetic Progress Gauges
- Track baseline sits at a thick 12px depth in dark midnight `#080E1E`.
- Active fill utilizes high-vis `#CCFF00` transitioning into `#34D399` along the vector path.
- Rounded circular thumb handle with a high-vis `#CCFF00` perimeter outline and midnight navy core.

### Form Inputs & Telemetry Adjusters
- Heavy capsule containers with integrated uppercase unit labels floating permanently on the right edge (e.g., `LBS`, `KG`, `TARGET BPM`).
- Focus state: The outer border snaps instantly from `#1B2E52` to `#CCFF00` with an accompanying subtle glow effect.

### Segmented Kinetic Switcher
- Inset pill track in `#080E1E` holding multiple pill segments. Active segment slides along the container with an animated spring transition, wrapped in `#0F1C33` with glowing chartreuse typography.
