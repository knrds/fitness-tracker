## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance sanctuary for central nervous system (CNS) restoration and biometric longevity. At the intersection of organic cellular vitality and clinical elite sports science, the aesthetic rejects the frantic, neon-overloaded tropes of typical fitness applications in favor of an understated, ultra-premium restorative sanctuary.

The visual style blends minimalist architectural discipline with deep obsidian dark-field immersion and subtle tactile glass boundaries. Interfaces evoke calm mastery, cellular repair, and neurological parasympathetic recovery:
- **Atmospheric Obsidian Depth:** Deep botanical dark backgrounds reduce ocular fatigue and support circadian alignment.
- **Architectural Typography:** Unconventional, sculptural display letterforms command attention for key metrics, anchored by balanced, highly readable sans-serif geometry for critical physiological data.
- **Subtle Organic Boundaries:** Micro-borders with organic slate and moss undertones carve clear hierarchy without jarring mechanical contrasts.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from deep-tissue recovery and chlorophyll-rich cellular renewal. 

- **Primary (`#34D399` - Mint Bio-Signal):** Serves as the central energetic pulse, highlighting active biometric states, ready-to-train signals, and prime touch targets.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Deep Emerald):** Represents parasympathetic equilibrium and sustained biological baseline, applied to charts, secondary confirmations, and steady indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#059669` - Forest Core):** Provides rich baseline accents, depth strokes, and subtle state indications.
- **Neutral Surface Ecosystem:**
  - Base Background: `#0A120E` (Deep forest obsidian)
  - Surface Container / Elevated Card: `#111E18` (Desaturated deep moss slate)
  - Surface High: `#162820` (Tactile interactive hover / active cards)
  - Outline & Boundaries: `#1A2F25` (Subtle organic boundary)
  - Primary Text: `#E2E8F0` (Cool neutral clean text)
  - Muted Text: `#8B9D93` (Subdued chlorophyll slate)

## Typography

The type system pairs **Syne**—an expressive, avant-garde display typeface with sculptural geometry—with **Manrope**, an engineered, highly legible geometric sans-serif for rigorous biometric reads.

- **Syne** is reserved for high-impact metric values, recovery scores, and major structural headlines. Its distinctive forms elevate biometric balance from clinical dryness to performance luxury.
- **Manrope** powers body copy, real-time physiological logs, labels, and micro-copy, providing structural clarity and high legibility across dense data dashboards and variable ambient lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (reducing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile), using an intentional 8pt spatial baseline to maintain visual stillness.

- **Margins & Safe Boundaries:** Desktop experiences leverage generous `2.5rem` outer canvas padding to foster a focused, meditative focus area. Mobile collapses gracefully to `1.25rem` to prioritize screen real estate without touching physical device boundaries.
- **Rhythm & Breathing Room:** Restorative interfaces require high internal whitespace to prevent cognitive strain. Section gaps and biometric card stacks use `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) for major functional groupings, while component internals use `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-sm` (`0.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on harsh dropshadows or skeuomorphic bevels. Instead, elevation uses **tonal layer stacking** combined with **subtle organic edge boundaries** and **ambient bio-luminescent diffusion**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** `#0A120E`.
- **Layer 1 (Card / Rest Surface):** `#111E18` enclosed by a 1px solid border of `#1A2F25`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating / Active State):** `#162820` with an ambient glow of `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modal / Overlays):** `#111E18` at 92% opacity with a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a 1px perimeter border of `#34D399` at 20% opacity.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined geometric curve scale calibrated at level `2` (0.5rem base radius). This produces balanced curvature that softens the dense technicality of biometric data without losing modern scientific discipline:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`):** Applied to inputs, buttons, chips, and micro-indicators.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, `1rem`):** Standard for cards, biometric stat containers, and sensor feed tiles.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`rounded-xl`, `1.5rem`):** Reserved for floating modals, high-level dashboards, and biometric summary sheets.
- **Circular (`rounded-full`):** Reserved solely for state dots, avatars, and circular metric gauges (HRV rings and CNS recovery loops).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#34D399`, text in `#0A120E` (Syne or bold Manrope), with `0.5rem` border-radius. On hover, shifts to `#10B981` with a subtle diffuse glow: `0 0 20px rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background in transparent moss slate `#111E18`, border `1px solid #1A2F25`, text in `#E2E8F0`. On hover, the border brightens to `#34D399` and the background transitions to `#162820`.
- **Ghost:** Borderless with `#8B9D93` text, transitioning to `#34D399` with background tint on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact capsules for state classification (e.g., "Parasympathetic Dominant", "CNS Primed", "REM Deficit").
- Rendered with background `#162820`, text `#34D399`, border `1px solid #1A2F25`, and `0.5rem` rounded corners.

### Biometric Cards
- Structured with surface `#111E18`, subtle `1px solid #1A2F25` outline, and `1rem` corner rounding.
- Internal padding set consistently to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Display type (Syne) highlights key numerical indexes (e.g., HRV `84ms`, Readiness `96%`), supported by clean uppercase Manrope metadata labels (`label-sm`).

### Input Fields
- Dark input wells (`#0A120E`) nested within `#111E18` cards.
- Border `1px solid #1A2F25`, text `#E2E8F0`, placeholder `#8B9D93`.
- Active focus transitions the border to `#34D399` with a subtle inset glow `0 0 0 1px #34D399`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base control frame in `#111E18` with a 1px border of `#1A2F25`.
- Active selected state transitions the frame fill to `#34D399` with the interior icon or pip rendered in `#0A120E`.

### Physiological Gauges & Recovery Rings
- Circular recovery visualizers utilizing dual-arc tracks: background track in `#1A2F25`, active value track rendered in a continuous gradient from `#10B981` to `#34D399`.
- Central index counter rendered in Syne display font.
